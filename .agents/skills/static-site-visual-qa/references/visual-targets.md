# Visual Targets

## Viewports

Use these default viewports unless the task calls for more:

- Desktop: 1440 x 1000
- Tablet: 768 x 1024
- Mobile: 390 x 844
- Small mobile: 360 x 740 for dense checkout or navigation changes

## Priority Pages

Tier 1:

- `/index.html`
- `/boutique.html`
- `/checkout.html`
- `/brut-tradition.html`
- `/brut-rose.html`
- `/demi-sec.html`

Tier 2:

- `/cuvees.html`
- `/livraison-paiement.html`
- `/maison.html`
- `/terroir.html`
- `/avis-clients.html`
- `/cadeaux.html`

Tier 3:

- Blog pages, legal pages, thank-you pages, and admin pages unless touched.

## Checks

Desktop:

- Header and hero composition look intentional.
- Product media is not blurry, stretched, or awkwardly cropped.
- Sections have enough whitespace and hierarchy.
- CTA treatment is calm and visible.
- Footer and legal reassurance are readable.

Mobile:

- Navigation is reachable and not crowded.
- Hero content does not hide the next section completely.
- Product cards and price blocks do not overflow.
- Buttons and quantity controls are thumb-friendly.
- Text does not overlap images or adjacent content.
- Sticky or fixed UI does not block checkout actions.

## Screenshot Naming

When saving screenshots, prefer:

`artifacts/visual-qa/{page}-{viewport}.png`

Examples:

- `artifacts/visual-qa/index-mobile.png`
- `artifacts/visual-qa/boutique-desktop.png`
