# SEO Rubric

## Page Intent

Match metadata and headings to the page intent:

- Homepage: brand, house, Champagne from Channes / Cote des Bar.
- Boutique and product pages: cuvee discovery, buying confidence, price and format clarity.
- Maison / terroir / vigneron pages: authenticity, family house, vineyard work, local relevance.
- Blog pages: educational search intent and internal links toward relevant cuvees.
- Legal and checkout pages: clarity and trust, not SEO expansion.

## Required Checks

For each SEO-sensitive page:

- Exactly one H1.
- Unique title that names the page and brand where useful.
- Meta description that reads naturally and is page-specific.
- Canonical URL on the expected production domain.
- Valid JSON-LD when present.
- Internal links point to existing pages and anchors.
- Images include width, height, and appropriate alt text.
- Sitemap includes indexable public pages and excludes utility/admin pages when appropriate.

## Structured Data

Prefer conservative schema that reflects visible content. Do not invent:

- Review ratings not shown on the page.
- Offer availability not backed by commerce behavior.
- Organization facts not present elsewhere.
- Shipping promises beyond `livraison-paiement.html` and CGV.

## Copy Tone

Use French that sounds like a premium independent Champagne house:

- Specific, calm, and sensory when relevant.
- No aggressive urgency.
- No generic luxury filler.
- No keyword repetition that breaks the page rhythm.
