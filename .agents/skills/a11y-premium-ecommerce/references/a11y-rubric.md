# Accessibility Rubric

## Static HTML Checks

Check:

- `html` has a `lang` attribute.
- Each page has one H1 and coherent heading order.
- Images have useful `alt` text unless decorative.
- Buttons and links have accessible names.
- Forms have labels or explicit accessible names.
- Required fields and errors are understandable.
- Dialogs, drawers, menus, and notices have appropriate semantics.

## Keyboard Checks

For browser validation:

- Tab order follows the visual flow.
- Focus is always visible.
- Menus and modals can be opened and closed by keyboard.
- Escape closes overlays when appropriate.
- No focus trap occurs outside intentional modals.
- Checkout can be completed or abandoned without mouse-only steps.

## Ecommerce-Specific Checks

Users must be able to understand:

- Product name, format, price, and quantity.
- Cart totals and shipping information.
- Legal terms acceptance.
- Payment handoff and confirmation status.
- Errors and unavailable states.

## Premium UI Checks

Accessible states should match the brand:

- Focus rings are visible but refined.
- Error text is direct and reassuring.
- Disabled states are understandable.
- Motion is restrained and does not hide content.
